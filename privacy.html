Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Atlas Scout TikTok Publisher ("the app") is a desktop client that lets its owner authorize TikTok and publish approved videos on their behalf. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. The app is operated privately by its owner and is not offered to the general public.

Information we collect

The app stores only the data needed to authenticate with TikTok and publish approved content:

  • TikTok authorization tokens — the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues after you approve the connection
  • Basic TikTok account identity returned by TikTok (such as your open ID, display name, and avatar)
  • Video files and captions prepared for posting
  • Publish status and related logs used to confirm a post succeeded and to troubleshoot errors

The app does not collect your TikTok password. Authorization happens through TikTok's own login and consent screen.

How we use information

  • To authenticate with TikTok and keep your session valid
  • To upload and publish videos and captions you have approved
  • To report publish status and diagnose failures

The app uses your information only to provide these functions. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

How information is shared

The app shares data only with TikTok, and only as needed to publish on your behalf, through TikTok's official API. TikTok's handling of that data is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy. The app does not sell personal data and does not share it with any other third parties.

Data retention

Authorization tokens, account identity, prepared media, captions, and logs are stored locally and kept only as long as needed to operate the app. Prepared media and captions can be removed once a post is complete, and logs are retained only for troubleshooting.

Your choices and data deletion

You can remove your data at any time:

  • Revoke access — disconnect the app from your TikTok account at any time in your TikTok settings under Security & permissions → Manage app permissions. This invalidates the app's tokens.
  • Delete stored data — remove the app's locally stored tokens, media, captions, and logs, which erases all data the app holds.
  • Request deletion — contact us at the address below and we will delete any data associated with your authorization.

Data security

Data is stored locally on the owner's device and transmitted to TikTok over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access to the device and its stored data is limited to the owner.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under the age required by TikTok's own terms.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
